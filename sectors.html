The UK veterinary sector is under pressure, and reception is the bottleneck.

Consolidation, staff shortages, and rising client expectations have turned front-of-house into the weakest link. Most practices are losing revenue before a vet even picks up a phone.

The struggles

Missed calls and lost revenue

30–40% of peak-hour calls go unanswered, each one a lost appointment worth £80–£200.

Reception staff burnout and turnover

Juggling phones, walk-ins, and PMS updates drives 32.5% annual turnover. New hires take 3–6 months to become effective.

Recall compliance below 30%

Manual recall via letters and sporadic calls leaves 70–85% of vaccination and wellness reminders unanswered.

Lapsed clients invisible in the PMS

Thousands of 12+ month dormant clients sit in the system, representing tens of thousands in lost annual revenue.

Clinician time consumed by admin

Vets spend 60–90 minutes per day on SOAP notes alone, eroding time that should go to clinical care.

Optical practices are losing patients to recall gaps and retail competition.

With high-street chains, online retailers, and NHS contract pressures squeezing margins, independent and small-group optical practices need every patient to come back and every visit to convert.

The struggles

Recall compliance stuck at 40–50%

Two-year test intervals make patients easy to lose. Less than half return on time with manual recall.

Did-not-attend rates eroding chair time

10–15% DNA rates are common. Most practices don't follow up at all, or do so days later.

Private dispensing revenue under pressure

Online retailers undercut on price and patients leave after eye tests without purchasing in-store.

Reception overwhelmed with routine queries

Phone lines clogged with opening hours, NHS eligibility, and contact lens queries that don't need a human.

No systematic approach to patient retention

Patients drift to competitors not because of poor care, but because nobody asked them to stay.

How Clinevo solves it
✓

Automated recall across SMS, email, and WhatsApp

Personalised, multi-touch reminders with one-tap rebooking. Compliance jumps to 70–85%.

✓

No-show recovery fills empty testing slots

Automated follow-up within minutes of a DNA. Offers rebooking and fills from the waitlist.

✓

AI receptionist handles routine calls

Opening hours, NHS eligibility, and contact lens reorders all handled instantly by voice AI.

✓

Post-visit follow-up drives dispensing and reviews

Automated check-ins prompt dispensing purchases, Google reviews, and next-test rebooking.

✓

Lapsed patient reactivation recovers lost revenue

Targeted campaigns bring back 15–25% of overdue patients within 90 days.

UK dental practices are drowning in admin and bleeding revenue through empty chairs.

With NHS contract pressures, rising patient expectations, and the largest clinical workforce in UK primary care, dental is the market where operational AI has the biggest commercial impact.

The struggles

Failed-to-attend rates of 8–15%

Each missed appointment costs £60–£150. Most practices send one SMS reminder, if that, and accept the loss.

Plan membership underperforming

Less than 30% of eligible patients convert to dental plans because the conversation rarely happens at the right moment.

Phone lines saturated during peak hours

Monday mornings spike 40%+ above average. Patients hear engaged tones and book elsewhere.

Hygienist utilisation below capacity

Poor recall and no waitlist management leave hygienists with 15–25% empty chair time weekly.

No structured patient reactivation

Patients who miss their 6-month check-up rarely get follow-up, and after 12 months, they're effectively lost.

How Clinevo solves it
✓

Automated follow-up the moment a patient FTAs, offering rebooking, contacts the waitlist, recovers 20–40%.

✓

AI prompts plan membership at the right moment

✓

AI receptionist eliminates missed calls

Every call answered in under 1.5s, 24/7. Peak-hour capacity is no longer a constraint.

✓

Recall automation drives hygiene bookings

Multi-channel reminders for check-ups and hygiene with one-tap booking, pushing compliance to 70–85%.

✓

Lapsed patient campaigns bring revenue back

Targeted reactivation segmented by treatment history and value, deployed automatically via SMS and email.
