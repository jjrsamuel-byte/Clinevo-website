Our story

From frustration to foundation.

Clinevo was born from a simple observation: clinical practices across the UK are drowning in admin. Phones ring unanswered. Recall lists go untouched. Clinicians spend more time on paperwork than patients.

We saw AI tools being sold to practices that did not understand them, by vendors who did not understand clinics. The result was wasted budgets, frustrated teams, and technology gathering dust.

So we built something different. A consultancy that starts with your practice, your workflows, your team, your commercial goals, and works backwards to find where AI genuinely makes a difference. No jargon. No overselling. Just measurable outcomes.

Our purpose

Give clinicians their time back.

Every minute a vet, optometrist, or dentist spends on hold, chasing recalls, or writing notes is a minute they are not spending with patients. That is the problem we exist to solve.

We believe AI should be invisible to the patient and transformative for the team. It should answer the phone before it rings out. It should send the recall before the client forgets. It should write the notes while the clinician focuses on care.

Our purpose is not to sell technology. It is to make clinical practices more efficient, more profitable, and better places to work, using AI as the tool, not the goal.

"We measure success by one thing: did the practice get a better outcome than before we started?"

Our mission

Make AI work for every clinical practice in the UK.

There are over 30,000 veterinary, optical, and dental practices in the UK. Most are independent or small group operations. Most are facing the same challenges: rising costs, staff shortages, missed revenue, and growing admin burden.

Our mission is to make clinical AI accessible, practical, and commercially viable for every one of them. Not just the large corporate groups with dedicated IT teams and six-figure technology budgets.

We do this by combining deep clinical understanding with proven AI implementation methodology. Education first, so teams understand what they are adopting. Strategy second, so investment is directed where it matters most. Implementation third, so outcomes are measurable from day one.
