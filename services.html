Frequently asked questions

What AI services does Clinevo offer for clinical practices?

Clinevo offers a range of AI services designed specifically for veterinary, optical, and dental practices. These include AI receptionist setup and managed service, urgency routing, web chat and WhatsApp AI, no-show recovery, appointment optimisation, recall automation, lapsed client reactivation, care plan upsell prompts, post-visit follow-up, seasonal campaign automation, compliance reporting, and the Clinevo Academy training programme.

Do I need to sign a long-term contract?

No. Clinevo does not require long-term lock-in contracts. Services are provided on a monthly basis so you can scale up, scale down, or stop at any time. The goal is to deliver measurable results that make you want to stay, not contractual obligations.

Can I choose individual services or do I need the full package?

You can choose individual services. Most practices start with the AI receptionist and add other services as they see results. Clinevo will recommend the combination that delivers the best return for your specific practice, but the choice is always yours.

How does Clinevo's AI receptionist handle urgent calls?

The AI receptionist uses urgency routing to detect emergency and time-sensitive calls. These are flagged and escalated immediately to the appropriate team member or on-call clinician, while routine enquiries are handled automatically. The routing rules are configured to match your practice's specific triage protocols.

What results can I expect from recall automation?

Practices using Clinevo's recall automation typically see a significant increase in recall compliance rates. Automated reminders reach clients through their preferred channels — SMS, email, or WhatsApp — at the right intervals, recovering appointments that would otherwise be missed.

Does Clinevo integrate with my existing practice management system?

Clinevo is designed to work alongside your existing practice management system, not replace it. During the discovery phase, we assess your current tech stack and configure integrations so that data flows smoothly between your PMS and our AI tools without disrupting your team's workflow.
